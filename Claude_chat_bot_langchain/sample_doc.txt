TechNova Solutions - Employee Handbook (Sample)

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal.
Unused leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave, capped at 10 days per year, and requires a
medical certificate for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require director-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM local time.
The company provides a one-time home office stipend of 15,000 INR for remote setup.
Part-time employees receive 9 days of paid annual leave per year, pro-rated based on hours worked.

SECTION 3: REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Business travel expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days of submitting receipts.
Meals during business travel are capped at 1,200 INR per day.
Internet and phone bill reimbursement for remote employees is capped at 1,500 INR per month.
All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October.
Reviews are based on goal completion, peer feedback, and manager assessment.
Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" are eligible for a performance bonus of up to 15%
of annual base salary. Salary revisions typically follow the April review cycle.

SECTION 5: PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION
New employees undergo a 90-day probation period from their date of joining.
During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days written notice.
Confirmation of employment is based on a review by the reporting manager at day 80.
Probation may be extended by up to 30 days if performance goals are not fully met.

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain confidentiality of proprietary company information.
Conflicts of interest, including outside employment, must be disclosed to HR in writing.
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action,
up to and including termination, following an internal investigation.
